Frequently Asked Questions

1. What products can I find at Toy Haven?
Toy Haven offers figurines, toys, board games and diecast cars, including collectibles from brands such as Hot Wheels, LEGO
2. How do I add a product to my cart?
Click “Add to Cart” on the product you want. Open your cart to check the items, change quantities and continue to checkout.localStorage. No external database or server is required, meaning the entire project can be run directly via VS Code Live Server or double-clicked as local files.
Can I save products to buy later?
Yes. Click the heart icon on a product to add it to your wishlist. You can view your saved products on the Wishlist page.
Is there a student promo discount?
Yes! Use promo code STUDENT10 in your cart to receive a simulated 10% student discount. Orders over £30 also automatically qualify for Free Standard Delivery.
Is the design mobile friendly and accessible?
Yes. The site follows semantic HTML5 guidelines, includes ARIA labels, responsive grids, touch-friendly interactive targets, keyboard focus-visible outlines, and a mobile hamburger menu.
How can I contact Toy Haven?
Visit the Support page and fill in your name, email, subject and message. Click “Send” to submit the form.
